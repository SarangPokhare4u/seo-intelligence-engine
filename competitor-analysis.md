# Competitor SEO Intelligence

## Identify organic competitors

Business competitors and organic competitors differ. Organic competitors are the domains that repeatedly rank for the site's priority keyword clusters, including publishers, marketplaces, directories, and forums.

Method: for 10-30 priority keywords, record top-10 domains (observed SERPs or tool export). Count appearances per domain. Domains appearing on 20%+ of the set are primary organic competitors [INT threshold]. Label the data source and date.

## Analysis dimensions

| Dimension | What to look at | Data source |
|---|---|---|
| Keyword overlap | Shared rankings, gaps (they rank, site doesn't) | Tool export or observed SERPs |
| Ranking pages | Which page types win (product, guide, comparison, tool) | Observed |
| Architecture | URL structure, hubs, navigation depth | Site visit, sitemap |
| Cluster strategy | Pillars/clusters, internal links between them | Site visit |
| Informational vs commercial coverage | Ratio of guides vs money pages | Sitemap sample |
| Differentiation | Unique data, tools, expertise, proof | Content review |
| Conversion paths | CTAs, demo flows, gated assets | Site visit |
| Referring domains | Who links and why | Ahrefs/Semrush/Moz export only |
| SERP feature ownership | Who wins observed features | Observed |

Mark any dimension without data as `not assessed: <reason>`.

## Opportunity classes

- **Quick wins**: site ranks 5-20 or has impressions; small on-page/internal-link changes.
- **High-conversion opportunities**: BOFU terms competitors win with comparison/pricing/use-case pages the site lacks.
- **Strategic content gaps**: missing clusters central to the business.
- **Existing content improvements**: thin or outdated pages that match intent.
- **Long-term authority investments**: original research, tools, digital PR.

## Report structure

Summary; competitor set with evidence; overlap/gap table; page-type insights; opportunity table (class, keyword cluster, recommended action, URL, priority, evidence); unavailable data list.
