## Research before writing

1. Reader/ICP: role, seniority, knowledge level, what they must decide or do.
2. Business goal and conversion action.
3. Topic and dominant search intent (SERP-confirmed when possible).
4. Keyword set: primary, secondary, questions (see keyword-research.md).
5. Review top-ranking pages: structure, depth, format, what they cover well.
6. Gaps: missing questions, outdated data, no first-hand evidence, no worked examples, weak comparisons.
7. Verify claims against primary sources; collect citations with dates.
8. Find an original angle: proprietary data, practitioner experience, customer stories (real and approved), a framework, a calculation, a counterpoint.
9. Write the brief ([templates/content-brief.md](../templates/content-brief.md)).
10. Only then draft.

## Writing standards

- Answer first, then explain. Each section earns its place.
- Specific over general: numbers with sources, named processes, real constraints.
- Reader's vocabulary; define terms once.
- Short paragraphs; varied sentence length; scannable headings that say something.
- Natural keyword use; secondary terms appear where they describe the content.
- Claims about the user's product come only from user-supplied or site-published facts; unknowns become `[CONFIRM: ...]` placeholders.
- First-hand experience must be real. If none exists, do not imply it; ask for it or use attributable expert input.
- Respect brand rules supplied by the user or by a brand skill (they override the defaults here).

## Banned patterns

Generic openers ("In today's fast-paced world", "In the ever-evolving landscape", "Are you looking for"); filler transitions ("Moreover", "Furthermore" chains, "It's worth noting that"); hype words ("game-changer", "revolutionary", "cutting-edge", "seamless", "robust", "innovative", "unlock", "delve", "leverage" as filler); unsupported superlatives ("the best", "#1"); invented statistics, testimonials, quotes, case studies, customer names, or awards; FAQ sections added only for SEO; repetitive "X is Y. Y is important." structures; rewriting competitor content.

Do not try to evade AI-content detectors; aim for accurate, original, useful writing.

## Format playbooks

| Type | Core job | Must include |
|---|---|---|
| Homepage | Who it's for, what it does, why believe, next step | Value proposition, proof, product paths, CTA |
| B2B SaaS landing | Convert a defined buyer | Problem, outcome, how it works, proof, objections, integrations, CTA |
| Product page | Explain one product | Capabilities tied to jobs, specs, use cases, proof, CTA |
| Service page | Explain an engagement | Scope, process, deliverables, who it's for, pricing approach, CTA |
| Blog article | Answer a query fully | Direct answer, depth, examples, sources, next step |
| Pillar | Own a topic | Broad coverage, links to all cluster pages, summary tables |
| Cluster article | Answer one subtopic | Link to pillar and commercial page |
| Comparison | Help choose | Fair criteria, table, when each option fits, disclosure of bias |
| Use-case page | Map product to a job | Scenario, workflow, outcome, proof |
| Industry solution | Map to a vertical | Industry problems, terminology, compliance, proof |
| Case study | Prove results | Real customer (approved), baseline, actions, measured outcome, quote (approved) |
| FAQ section | Answer real recurring questions | Questions from sales/support/PAA; concise answers (FAQ rich results are deprecated) |
| Resource center | Organize content | Taxonomy, filters, featured content |
| Educational guide | Teach a process | Steps, visuals, templates |
| Thought leadership | Argue a point of view | Thesis, evidence, counterargument, implication |
| Glossary | Define terms | Definition first, context, related terms, links |
| Local service page | Serve a real location | Genuine local details, NAP, service area, local proof |
| E-commerce category | Help browse | Short intro, filters, buying guidance, internal links |
| Product description | Sell one SKU | Specs, benefits, use, care, unique details (not manufacturer copy) |
| Refresh/rewrite | Improve existing | Preserve meaning and facts, update data, fill gaps, keep ranking URL |

## Required deliverable package

1 strategy summary; 2 primary keyword; 3 secondary keywords; 4 search intent; 5 target audience; 6 SEO title; 7 meta description; 8 suggested URL; 9 H1; 10 complete publish-ready content; 11 heading hierarchy; 12 internal links (anchor -> URL); 13 external citations (URL + date accessed); 14 image recommendations + alt text; 15 structured data (supported types only); 16 CTA; 17 conversion rationale; 18 differentiation summary; 19 fact-check status per claim; 20 SEO QA checklist result.

## Fact-checking protocol

For every statistic, date, named claim, or quote: source URL, publisher, publication date, exact figure. Status: `verified (opened this session)`, `user-supplied`, `needs confirmation`. Remove or flag anything that cannot be verified. Prefer primary sources (government, standards bodies, original studies) over aggregator "statistics" pages.

## Brand voice handling

If the user has a brand/voice skill or guide, load it and follow it over these defaults. Record which voice rules were applied in the QA section.
