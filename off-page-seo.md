## Policy boundaries

Prohibited (link spam under Google spam policies): buying/selling links that pass ranking credit; links in exchange for free products without qualification; excessive link exchanges; PBNs; automated link creation; widget/footer links at scale; guest posts or press releases with keyword-rich followed links placed for ranking; comment/forum spam; requiring a link as part of a contract term. Sponsored placements are allowed only with `rel="sponsored"` (or `nofollow`). Bing's guidelines also prohibit link schemes and AI manipulation.

Disavow is for real manual-action risk or known paid/spam links the site created, not routine cleanup.

## Opportunity types

| Type | When it fits | Asset needed |
|---|---|---|
| Digital PR (data-led) | Site can publish original data | Survey, proprietary data analysis, index |
| Expert commentary | Executives with real expertise | Bio, availability, quotable insight |
| Unlinked brand mentions | Brand mentioned without link | Mention URL + polite request |
| Resource pages | Curated lists in the niche | A genuinely useful resource |
| Broken link replacement | Dead resource on relevant page, with a real equivalent | Equivalent or better resource |
| Associations / professional directories | Membership is real | Membership |
| Partners, integrations, customers | Real relationship | Partner page, case study |
| Events / sponsorships | Real participation | Sponsored links tagged `sponsored` |
| Podcasts / webinars / bylines | Editorial value | Topic pitch; links follow publication policy |
| Original tools / templates | Linkable utility | Tool page |
| Competitor backlink gap | Data available | Export from Ahrefs/Semrush/Moz |

## Prospect evaluation rubric (score 0-2 each, max 18)

Topical relevance; editorial quality (real authors, edits, original reporting); real audience (comments, social reach, newsletter, verified traffic data if available); publication credibility; site legitimacy (not a link farm: no "write for us" for any topic, no sitewide sponsored posts, no casino/pharma spam); geographic relevance; placement context (in-body, relevant page); link policy (editorial, not pay-to-play); spam risk (inverse).

Exclude anything that sells followed links or exists mainly to sell guest posts.

## Research methods

Run with WebSearch or a browser, then open and inspect each prospect (never list unopened sites):

- `"<industry term>" "resources"` / `intitle:resources "<topic>"`
- `"<brand name>" -site:<brand domain>` (unlinked mentions; verify no link on page)
- `"<topic>" "expert" OR "according to" site:<publication>` (who quotes experts)
- `"<topic>" "statistics" <year>` (data-roundup pages that cite sources)
- Trade associations: `"<industry> association" members`
- Journalists: publication author pages, contributor guidelines pages
- Competitor gap: export referring domains for 2-3 organic competitors; keep domains linking to 2+ competitors but not the site

Contact data: only publicly listed emails or contact forms found on the site/author page; record the URL where found. Otherwise `contact: not publicly listed`. Never guess email patterns.

## Outreach rules and email patterns

- Personalize with a specific, verifiable reference to their work.
- Lead with value to their readers; no requests for "dofollow".
- One follow-up after 5-7 business days [INT]; stop after that.
- Comply with CAN-SPAM/GDPR basics: identify sender, honor opt-out.
- Draft only. Sending requires explicit user authorization.

Pattern (journalist data pitch):
```
Subject: <specific finding> in <their beat>
Hi <name>, your <date> piece on <topic> noted <specific point>. We analyzed <dataset, n, period> and found <finding>. Method and full data: <link>. Happy to share the raw table or a comment from <expert, title>.
<sign-off with name, title, company>
```

## Metrics and caveats

DR/DA/AS/TF are vendor estimates, not Google metrics. Report referring domains, relevance, and traffic quality from the site's analytics (referral sessions, conversions) over raw link counts. Industry correlation studies (e.g., brand mentions correlating with AI Overview visibility, Ahrefs 2025) are correlation only.
